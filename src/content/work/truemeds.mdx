## The problem

TrueMeds sells prescription medicine with a substitute recommendation attached: the same molecule, a different manufacturer, at a lower price. The value to the customer is explicit and quantified on screen. Customers reached that recommendation and hesitated anyway.

<ProblemFrame
  visible="Customers dropped out around the substitute recommendation, close to the point of purchase."
  underlying="Discovery was never the problem — the recommendation was already in front of them. What was missing was confidence at the moment of the decision. A substitute asks someone to overrule a familiar medicine, a prescription in their hand, and an absent doctor, on their own judgement."
  challenge="Design for confidence at the point of the substitute decision, rather than for a clearer explanation of the saving."
